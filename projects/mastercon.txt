name   = MasterConnector: a DB-37 Master-Connector to Connect Any Retro PC to a KVM Box
# finished | in-progress | future | proprietary
status = in-progress
# hardware | software | hybrid
type   = hardware
tech   = Hardware Design

desc   = I dislike having to deal with so many cables when swapping out machines I work with regularly. So, I have devised a way to use a 37-pin connector to carry everything other than power to a desktop. By having male plugs plugged into each desktop (or laptop) that are adapted to a male 37-pin, and a box with female jacks and a female 37-pin, any equipped PC can be swapped out with a single connector: mouse, keyboard, network, display, etc. I have also developed a way to use an Arduino to adapt the AT PS/2 keyboard protocol to XT for particularly old devices, and added switches that select keyboard/mouse protocol, and a switch to enable or disable ethernet at the hardware level.
